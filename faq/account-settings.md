# Account settings

**Where do I change my account settings?**
Open your own profile from the user menu → the **Settings** tab. From there you manage your
display name, avatar, password, email/phone and linked sign-in accounts. See
[docs/16](../docs/16-account-settings.md).

**Can I change my username?**
The username is fixed, but you can set a friendly **display (alias) name** shown in its place.

**How do I change my password?**
In the **Security** section, enter your current password and a new one. 4PM sends a
**confirmation email** — the change takes effect once you confirm via the link, not immediately.

**How do I change my email or phone?**
One field at a time, with verification: changing **email** sends a one-time code to your **phone**;
changing **phone** sends a link to your **email**. A pending change shows a **Cancel** button until
you confirm.

**How do I connect or disconnect Google/GitHub?**
In **Linked accounts**, connect a provider to sign in with it, or disconnect one you don't use —
you just can't remove your **last** sign-in method. Registered via SSO with no password? Add one
here for a password login too.

**I'm blocked by an IP restriction.**
Your account may have an **IP allowlist**. Only an ADMIN (or a manager of your account) can edit
it — a regular user can't change their own. Ask an ADMIN.

**Can an admin reset my password for me?**
Yes — an ADMIN can reset another user's password directly (which logs that user out). ADMINs can't
reset the root user's or their own this way; they use the Security section.

**How do I turn on two-factor authentication?**
Profile › **Security**: set up an authenticator app or register a passkey, and save the recovery codes.
See [docs/02](../docs/02-accounts-and-login.md#two-factor-authentication-2fa).

**Why did the app lock itself?**
Your organization locks idle sessions after a set time. Unlock with your password or your **PIN**
(4–12 digits, set in your profile's Security panel).

**How do I change the language or switch to dark mode?**
Open the settings menu: language (English, Tiếng Việt, 日本語, 中文), appearance (light / dark / system)
and theme (Default, Flat, 8-bit).
