# Chat dock, memos & discussions

The **Chat dock** is the panel that slides in from the **Chat** tab on the right edge of every page. It has
three tabs — **Chats**, **Memo** and **Discuss** — so you can talk, take notes and answer discussions without
leaving the screen you're on. See [docs/31](../docs/31-research-announcements-and-chat.md).

## The dock

**How do I open it?**
Click the **Chat** tab on the right edge of the screen. Close it with the **Close** tab on the panel's edge or
**Esc**; whether it is open is remembered.

**What do the red dots mean?**
A red dot on **Chats** or **Discuss** means something new is waiting there; the same dot shows on the Chat tab
at the screen edge, so you notice it even when the dock is closed. **Discuss** also shows how many discussions
are waiting on you.

**What does the project filter do?**
On **Chats** and **Discuss** it narrows the list to one project. It lists every project you can see — ADMINs
see every project in the organization. The **go-to** button next to it opens that project's **Messenger** (on
Chats) or **Discussions** (on Discuss), or your project list when no project is picked.

**A tab is empty — how do I start?**
An empty Chats or Discuss tab links straight to the project's Messenger or Discussions (or to your projects)
so you can start a conversation there.

## Chats

**What's in Chats?**
Your direct and group conversations, per project — the same ones as the project's **Messenger**. Open one to
read and reply in the dock.

## Memo

**What are personal memos?**
Short private notes — reminders, things to check, ideas — that only **you** can see (not even an ADMIN sees
another person's memos). Each memo is filed under a project, can be checked off when done, and can be
attached to a book row or a My work item.

**How do I write one?**
Open **Memo**, type the note and add it. The project you're viewing is picked for you; change it in the
project field. ADMINs can file a memo under **any** project of the organization.

**How do I attach a memo to a row?**
On a User Todo, User Q&A, AI Todo or AI Done row, open the row's **⋯** menu (or the actions bar of an expanded
row) and choose **New memo** — or **My memos** to see the ones already on it. On Home › **My work**, use an
item's memo button. Those memos also appear in the Memo tab, with a link back to the row or item.

**How do I finish or remove a memo?**
Tick it to mark it done; use its delete button to remove it. The list is only as tall as your memos.

**Is there a limit?**
Your plan sets the maximum number of memo items in the organization; Home › **Usage** shows how many are used.

## Discuss

**What's in Discuss?**
The project **discussions** that address or mention you. You can read them, **reply**, **resolve** or
**reopen** them right in the dock. The count on the tab is how many wait on you.

**How is a discussion different from a chat?**
A **discussion** is a decision thread stored with the project (in its repo): it has a title, can be linked to
book rows, has tags and a due date, and is **resolved** when settled. A linked row can't be approved while
its discussion is open. A **chat** is just a conversation.

**Where do I start a discussion?**
From a book row (its **⋯** menu or the chat icon) or in the project's Content › **Discussions**. A finished
task in AI Done can be discussed too — and that discussion can be turned into a **follow-up request** in
User Todo.

**Where else do I see discussions waiting on me?**
Home › **My work** lists them with your other items. See [Tags, due dates & My work](./tags-and-my-work.md).
