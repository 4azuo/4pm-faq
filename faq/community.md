# Community (4rum)

**What is 4rum?**
Your organization's built-in feed/forum — post updates and discuss with everyone in your org. See
[docs/19](../docs/19-community.md).

**Where do I find it?**
Open **4rum** from the profile dropdown, or the **Community** widget on the Home overview.

**What can I do there?**
Read a timeline of posts, write posts in **Markdown** (code, quotes, Mermaid) with images and files,
**react**, **comment/reply**, **quote**, and edit or delete your own posts within your org's edit window
(with Undo).

**Can I tie a post to a project?**
Yes — a post **tagged to a project** also appears in that project's **Community** tab, so
project-specific discussion stays with the project.

**Who sees my post?**
Pick a scope: **project**, **organization** or **global**. Global posts need an ADMIN or PM to approve
them first (Pending queue). For curated write-ups, use the **Knowledge hub** (see
[marketplace](./marketplace.md)).

**I don't see the 4rum tab.**
4rum needs the **Max** plan or higher; the tab is hidden on other plans.

**Where are direct messages?**
In a project's Community tab › **Messenger**, or the **Chat** tab on the right edge of every page
(Pro plan and above). See [docs/31](../docs/31-research-announcements-and-chat.md).
