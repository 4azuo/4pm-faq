# Community (4rum)

**What is 4rum?**
Your organization's built-in feed/forum — post updates and discuss with everyone in your org. See
[docs/19](../docs/19-community.md).

**Where do I find it?**
Open **4rum** from the profile dropdown, or the **Community** widget on the Home overview.

**What can I do there?**
Read the latest org posts (newest first) and **create a post** (title + body).

**Can I tie a post to a project?**
Yes — a post **tagged to a project** also appears in that project's **Community** tab, so
project-specific discussion stays with the project.

**Is 4rum public?**
No — it's scoped to your **organization**. The public, cross-org place to share write-ups is the
**Knowledge hub** (see [marketplace](./marketplace.md)).

See also: [Community (4rum)](../docs/19-community.md).
