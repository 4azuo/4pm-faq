# AI Research, announcements & chat

Organization-wide tools for asking the AI, telling people things, and talking to each other.

## AI Research

Open the **Chat** panel on the right edge of any page and switch to its **Research** tab: ask the AI pool
any question and get a researched answer streamed live, kept as a browsable **history**. You can attach
images and files for the AI to read.

- Available on every plan; it runs on the organization's AI pool (Organization Settings › AI) and uses
  your quota.
- Sensitive, political, discriminatory or system-attack questions are declined.
- For questions about **how to use 4PM**, use **AI Help** instead (see [Get help](./12-getting-help.md)).

## Announcements

ADMINs, PMs and team leads can send a message to people in their scope from Home › **Announcements**
(Pro plan and above):

1. **Pick recipients** — any mix of users, teams and projects (ADMIN: the whole org; PM: their projects;
   team lead: their teams). You see how many people it will reach (and how many by email).
2. **Compose** — a subject and a Markdown body; choose **In-app** and/or **Email**. Save as a draft if
   you're not ready.
3. **Send** — recipients get it in their notifications and/or inbox; email replies go straight to you.

A **sent history** lets you reuse a past message. Each plan caps the recipients per day (Pro 500, Max
2 000).

## Messenger & the Chat dock

- In a project's **Community** tab, **Messenger** has direct chats and **group chats** organized in
  sessions (a session named after the project, with sub-sessions). Write with the rich editor (Enter
  sends, Shift+Enter adds a line), attach files, react, edit, delete (with undo), quote and forward to
  other conversations in the project. Each conversation has **Files** and **Links** tabs; groups have a
  **Members** tab.
- The **Chat** tab on the right edge of every page opens the **Chat dock**:
  - **Chats** — your conversations from all projects in one place, with search, a project filter (every
    project you can see; ADMINs see all) with a go-to button to the project's Messenger, and unread markers.
  - **Memo** — your personal memos (private to you), filed under a project — the one you're viewing is picked
    for you; ADMINs can file under any project. Memos added on a book row or a My work item show here too.
  - **Discuss** — the discussions that address or mention you: read, reply, resolve or reopen them in place.
    The tab counts what waits on you.
  - A red dot on Chats / Discuss (and on the edge tab) means something new. Empty tabs link to the project's
    Messenger or Discussions. See [Chat dock, memos & discussions](../faq/chat-dock-and-memos.md).
- The Chat panel's **Requests** tab lists your [change requests](./26-checklists.md#change-requests)
  and their outcome.
- **Presence:** an online / idle / offline dot on avatars across the app.
- ADMINs set how long authors may edit or delete messages and posts (Settings › Community, 60 minutes by
  default).

Chat needs the Pro plan or higher.

## Memo

Every user, team and project page has a **Memo** tab — notes with status, search, sort and paging. Personal
notes are private; team notes are shared with the team; project notes with the project. Attach images and
files; **Compose community post** turns selected notes into a 4rum post with AI.
