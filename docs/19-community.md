# Community (4rum)

**4rum** is your organization's built-in feed/forum — a lightweight place to post updates and
discuss with everyone in your org.

## Where to find it

Open **4rum** from the **profile dropdown**, or from the **Community** widget on the Home
overview.

## What you can do

- **Read** the feed as a timeline grouped by day; older posts load as you scroll.
- **Write posts in Markdown** (code, quotes, Mermaid diagrams) with a live preview, and attach images
  and files.
- **React** with emotions, **comment** and **reply**, **quote** a post (click a quote to jump to the
  original), and **edit / delete** your own posts within the time your org allows (60 minutes by
  default) — with **Undo**.
- **Pick a scope:** **project**, **organization** or **global**. Project posts also appear in that
  project's **Community** tab and its Dashboard news. **Global** posts need an ADMIN or PM to approve them
  (a **Pending** queue) before everyone sees them.

4rum needs the **Max** plan or higher; the tab is hidden otherwise.

## Messenger & chat

Inside a project's Community tab, **Messenger** has direct chats and group chats (organized in sessions),
with Markdown, attachments, reactions, edit/delete/undo and forwarding. The **Chat** tab on the right edge
of every page shows all your conversations across projects, with search and unread markers. Online / idle /
offline dots show who's around. (Chat needs the Pro plan or higher.)

4rum is a feed for conversation; for curated, reviewed write-ups use the
