# Community (4rum)

**4rum** is your organization's built-in feed/forum — a lightweight place to post updates and
discuss with everyone in your org.

## Where to find it

Open **4rum** from the **profile dropdown**, or from the **Community** widget on the Home
overview.

## What you can do

- **Read** the latest posts from across your organization, newest first. Each post shows its
  title, body, author and time.
- **Create a post** with a title and body.
- **Tag a post to a project** — a post tagged with a project also appears in that project's
  **Community** tab, so project-specific discussion stays with the project.

4rum is scoped to your **organization** (it's separate from the public
[Knowledge hub](./13-marketplace.md#knowledge-hub), which is for sharing write-ups across orgs).
