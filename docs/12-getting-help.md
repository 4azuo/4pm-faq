# Get help & support

4PM has two layers of help: an **in-app AI Help chat** for instant answers about using the
product, and **human email support** when you need a person.

## AI Help chat

Open the **Help** chat bubble in the app and ask a question in plain language — for example
"How do I pair a worker?" or "Why is my push blocked?".

How it works:

1. Your question is **queued** and answered by a dedicated 4PM AI support agent. You'll see your
   **position** in the queue if agents are busy ("waiting… #3").
2. An agent reads the official 4PM docs and FAQ and composes a **grounded answer**, referencing
   the relevant doc/FAQ where useful.
3. The answer **streams back** into the chat as it's written.

The Help chat is for **product usage** questions ("how do I use 4PM"). It answers from the
official documentation and won't expose admin-only features to non-admin users.

## When the AI can't help

- If the agent can't find a confident, grounded answer, it will **suggest escalating to a human**
  rather than guessing.
- You can also click **"email a human"** at any time to hand the conversation to human support.
- If no AI agent is available at that moment, you'll see a "temporarily unavailable — email
  support" message.

## Human email support

Escalating (or emailing support directly) opens a **support request** that goes to 4PM's support
queue. A human reviews it and replies by email. Your chat transcript is included so you don't have
to re-explain the context.

## Tips for faster answers

- Say **what you were doing** and **what happened** (the exact error message helps).
- Mention the area: pairing, projects, Git, billing, integrations, autonomous mode.
- For access problems, note your **role** — many blocks are missing-permission issues an ADMIN or
  PM can fix.

See also the FAQ topics in [`../faq`](../faq/) for quick self-service answers.
